# Story Mode Image Prompts & Continuity Bible

## 1. Character Continuity Reference

### Arjun (Protagonist)
- **Age & Role:** 20 years old, 3rd year Computer Science student in Puducherry, India. Building a bus-tracker app with Team Nimbus.
- **Physical Appearance:** Relatable young Indian man, warm brown eyes, neat short black hair, clean-shaven or light stubble, medium build.
- **Wardrobe:** Casual college clothing (simple cotton collared shirts in teal or navy, comfortable denim, college backpack).
- **Expression:** Thoughtful, occasionally distracted or tired in early scenes, relieved and engaged in hopeful scenes. Consistent across all scenes.

### Karthik (Best Friend & Teammate)
- **Age & Role:** 20 years old, warm, perceptive, empathetic, loyal friend.
- **Physical Appearance:** Curly hair, glasses, casual hoodies or college t-shirts.

### Amma (Arjun's Mother)
- **Role:** Caring mother in Puducherry, traditional cotton saree, gentle maternal presence.

### Dr. Nandhini (Wellness Centre Counsellor)
- **Role:** Professional, calm, empathetic counsellor at college wellness centre. Simple elegant kurta, thoughtful and respectful demeanor.

## 2. Visual Direction & Style Guidelines
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (1080×608 px) landscape composition.
- **Aesthetic:** Editorial digital illustration with cinematic lighting, restrained emotion, warm daylight, and atmospheric color grading.
- **Color Palette:** Deep Teal (`#0E9F8E`), Dark Teal (`#087F70`), Off-White (`#F6F8FA`), Navy (`#0F1B2D`), Amber (`#FFE2B8`), Soft Sky (`#BAE6FD`).
- **Strict Content Safeguards:** Zero depictions of substances, drug paraphernalia, pills, alcohol, bottles, or smoke. No sensationalism or shaming.
